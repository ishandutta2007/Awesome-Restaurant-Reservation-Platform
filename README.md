# Awesome-Restaurant-Reservation-Platform

## Top Restaurant Reservation Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Table Booking, Guest Management, Floor Plans, CRM, Direct Reservations & Restaurant Operations*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Restaurant Reservation Systems**. These tools help restaurants take online bookings, manage table inventory, reduce no-shows, build guest profiles, and (in many cases) reach diners through large consumer networks.

**Examples** include OpenTable, Resy, SevenRooms, Quandoo, Eat App, Hostme, Tock, TableCheck, ResDiary, Yelp Guest Manager, and Wisely (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Restaurant reservation software has solid self-hosted options. **TastyIgniter**, **OpenResto**, **SeatKit**, and related projects provide booking engines, table management, and ordering capabilities without per-cover network fees. This section is expanded with these tools.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com/)**  
  The largest global restaurant reservation network, combining diner discovery with restaurant-side table management, guest data, and marketing tools (typically subscription + per-cover fees).

- **[Resy](https://resy.com/)**  
  Upscale-focused reservation platform (American Express) known for prime-time inventory, notify lists, and strong presence in major US cities; integrating Tock inventory and features.

- **[SevenRooms](https://sevenrooms.com/)**  
  Reservation and guest-experience platform emphasizing direct bookings, deep CRM, marketing automation, and multi-venue operations for restaurants and hospitality groups.

- **[Quandoo](https://www.quandoo.com/)**  
  International reservation platform popular in Europe and other markets for connecting diners with restaurants and providing booking management tools.

- **[Eat App](https://restaurant.eatapp.co/)**  
  Flat-fee reservation and guest management platform focused on CRM, messaging, and direct bookings without traditional per-cover network fees.

- **[Hostme](https://www.hostmeapp.com/)**  
  Restaurant reservation and table-management solution aimed at streamlining front-of-house operations and online bookings.

- **[Tock](https://www.exploretock.com/)**  
  Reservation and ticketing platform especially strong for tasting menus, prepaid experiences, and high-demand fine dining (now combining with Resy under Amex).

- **[TableCheck](https://www.tablecheck.com/)**  
  Reservation and restaurant management platform with strong presence in Asia and growing international coverage.

- **[ResDiary](https://www.resdiary.com/)**  
  Reservation and table-management system used by restaurants for online bookings, floor plans, and guest communication.

- **[Yelp Guest Manager](https://www.yelp.com/guestmanager)**  
  Reservation and waitlist tools integrated with the Yelp ecosystem for discovery and guest management.

- **[Wisely](https://www.getwisely.com/)**  
  Guest management and reservation platform focused on CRM, waitlists, and personalized guest experiences.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[TastyIgniter](https://github.com/tastyigniter/TastyIgniter)**  
  Powerful open-source (MIT) online ordering, table reservation, and restaurant management system built on Laravel — supports menus, bookings, multiple locations, and customer-facing sites.

- **[OpenResto](https://github.com/karanshukla/openresto)**  
  Self-hosted, focused booking management system for restaurants, bars, and cafes. Real-time table holds, multi-restaurant support, floor sections, and a simple mobile-friendly booking UI.

- **[SeatKit](https://github.com/seatkit/seatkit)**  
  Open-source reservation management system designed for high-turnover, small-seat restaurants with visual table management and real-time staff collaboration.

- **[QloApps](https://github.com/Qloapps/QloApps)**  
  Open-source hotel and property booking engine (PMS + booking website) that can inspire or be adapted for hospitality-style reservation workflows.

- **[Self-hosted booking engines and calendar systems](https://github.com/)**  
  General open reservation/calendar projects that restaurants adapt for table inventory and time-slot management.

- **[Floor-plan and table-status open UIs](https://github.com/)**  
  Front-of-house visualization prototypes for real-time table status, often paired with open booking backends.

- **[SMS/email confirmation open workflows](https://github.com/)**

- **[Guest CRM and preference open stores](https://github.com/)**

- **[Waitlist and walk-in management open tools](https://github.com/)**

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Deploying TastyIgniter or OpenResto on a VPS for full ownership of the booking experience and guest data.
- Combining an open booking engine with a restaurant’s own website and marketing stack (no network fees).
- Using open form + calendar tools for very small venues with limited volume.
- Layering open analytics (Metabase, etc.) on reservation data for covers, no-show rates, and channel performance.
- Mobile-friendly progressive web apps built on open booking APIs for guest self-service.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Self-host **TastyIgniter** or **OpenResto** for online reservations, table management, and (where supported) ordering. Own the guest database, avoid per-cover network fees, and integrate with your existing website and POS. Ideal for independents and groups that prioritize direct bookings and data ownership. Large consumer networks (OpenTable, Resy), deep hospitality CRM (SevenRooms), and prepaid/experience booking (Tock) remain strengths of the commercial platforms, especially when discovery and fill rates from a big diner audience are critical.

## Disclaimer
- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Reservation systems handle personal data (names, contact details, sometimes payment). Open-source deployments must implement proper security, backups, GDPR/CCPA-style privacy practices, and reliable uptime — especially during peak service. Network platforms also involve contractual and fee considerations that affect restaurant economics.
- Always test booking flows thoroughly before relying on any system for live service.
